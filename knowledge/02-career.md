# Career journey

## Career overview and timeline
Ray's career has three chapters. June 2024 to November 2024: AI Software Developer intern at CMG Technologies (CMGI Sdn Bhd, Petaling Jaya, Selangor). January 2025 to August 2025: AI Software Developer and AI Team Lead at CMG Technologies. September 2025 to present: AI Solution Architect at Huawei Technologies, APAC Enterprise Business Group, Kuala Lumpur. Before that he studied a Bachelor of Computer Science (Data Science) at TARUMT from September 2021 to November 2024.

## AI Software Developer Intern at CMG Technologies (Jun 2024 – Nov 2024)
Ray joined CMG Technologies (CMGI), a company building healthcare and F&B point-of-sale systems, as an AI Software Developer intern in June 2024. He explored frontier AI when no mature agent framework existed yet: he enforced structured output and ReAct-style tool calling by hand, worked around models that lacked native function calling, and built multi-agent workflows on LangGraph. The first thing shipped was an internal help-desk WhatsApp chatbot so staff could check their ticket status instantly. He assisted with frontend development and backend integration for the mini program and gained hands-on experience with staging environments and Kubernetes-based deployment. He also built a blood-result analysis tool with a user-friendly UI for processing and visualising data, and worked on optimising its performance and backend logic.

## AI Team Lead at CMG Technologies (Jan 2025 – Aug 2025)
After the R&D phase, the founder formed a dedicated AI team and hand-picked Ray to lead it (title: AI Software Developer, Team Lead). The role was about people and delivery as much as code: stakeholder management across departments, project management and delivery ownership, and translating between business needs and AI. Work included the WhatsApp F&B customer-service chatbot, the internal no-code agent builder, the mini program back office (Next.js, React, Kubernetes), internal productivity tools, and self-hosting LLMs on GPU servers with vLLM, Ollama and llama.cpp, including KV-cache handling, for cost-efficient internal AI services. To pass probation he needed to understand the full software development lifecycle, which the Madam Kwan's mini program and his RunForPeace side project gave him.

## Internal productivity tools at CMG
Ray built a code-generation AI that converts large PostgreSQL tables (around 1,000 records) into JSON-formatted table documentation with column and field descriptions. He also developed internal prototypes that automate routine developer tasks and improve productivity.

## AI Solution Architect at Huawei (Sep 2025 – present)
At Huawei's APAC Enterprise group Ray's remit widened beyond writing code: he owns the technical solution end to end and puts it in front of clients. He builds the POC, demos it to clients and defends the technical design, and maps it to the Huawei product stack and NPU hardware, alongside Huawei's Head of AI. Projects: the MyGOV AI Assistant (government super app), Water-Treatment Plant SCADA AI, bank credit-paper automation (banking), and an internal sales intelligence platform and management tools.

## Speaking and judging
Ray represented Huawei as an invited judge and speaker at the AI Singapore Hackathon at the National University of Singapore (NUS). He evaluated student and startup teams on the judging panel and shared an industry perspective with the AI community on stage.
