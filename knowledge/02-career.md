# Career journey

## AI Software Developer Intern at CMG Technologies (Jun 2024 – Nov 2024)
Ray joined CMG Technologies (CMGI), a company building healthcare and F&B point-of-sale systems, as an AI Software Developer intern in June 2024. He explored frontier AI when no mature agent framework existed yet: he enforced structured output and ReAct-style tool calling by hand, worked around models that lacked native function calling, and built multi-agent workflows on LangGraph. The first thing shipped was an internal help-desk WhatsApp chatbot so staff could check their ticket status instantly. He also did mini-program frontend and backend integration, Kubernetes staging, and a blood-result analysis UI with data visualisation.

## AI Team Lead at CMG Technologies (Jan 2025 – Aug 2025)
After the R&D phase, the founder formed a dedicated AI team and hand-picked Ray to lead it. The role was about people and delivery as much as code: stakeholder management across departments, project management and delivery ownership, and translating between business needs and AI. Work included the WhatsApp F&B customer-service chatbot, the internal no-code agent builder, the Madam Kwan's mini-program back office (Next.js, Kubernetes), a code-generation AI that documents large PostgreSQL tables as JSON, and self-hosting LLMs on GPU servers with vLLM, Ollama and llama.cpp, including KV-cache handling.

## AI Solution Architect at Huawei (Sep 2025 – present)
At Huawei's APAC Enterprise group Ray's remit widened beyond writing code: he owns the technical solution end to end and puts it in front of clients. He builds the POC (turning a client need into a working, demo-ready prototype), demos it to clients and defends the technical design, and maps it to the Huawei stack. Projects include the MyGOV AI Assistant, Water-Treatment SCADA AI, AmBank credit-paper automation and an internal sales intelligence platform.

## Speaking and judging
Ray represented Huawei as an invited judge and speaker at the AI Singapore Hackathon at the National University of Singapore (NUS). He evaluated teams on the judging panel and shared an industry perspective with the AI community on stage.
