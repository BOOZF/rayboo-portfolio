# Projects at Huawei

## MyGOV AI Assistant
A government super-app POC for Malaysian citizens to check summonses, STR status and other agency services conversationally. Ray delivered end-to-end POC solution services: a production-level application integrating multiple government agencies across Malaysia, with an AI chatbot that has governance controls, knowledge-based responses and tool calling. He was the sole builder of the POC-ready React app UI and presented the live demo; Huawei's Head of AI pitched the product stack. The bid reached the final round of the bid. The client went with another provider for cloud services but adopted Ray's UI, UX flow and design in their build — they lost on infrastructure, not on the solution.

## Water-treatment SCADA AI
For a government water-treatment plant, abnormal incidents arrive as floods of SCADA alarms that engineers traced by hand through sensor data and past incident reports. Ray built an AI-powered root-cause-analysis (RCA) workflow on Dify to detect anomalies and operational failures: he designed complex multi-step Dify workflows that combine SCADA sensor data, alarm logs and historical incident records, and implemented LLM + RAG to identify probable root causes and recommend corrective actions to plant operators. Automating anomaly detection and root-cause investigation through Dify AI orchestration improves real-time monitoring and incident response. He also supported the solution design and demoed it to the client. Goals: less manual troubleshooting, more accurate fault diagnosis and faster incident response.

## Bank credit-paper AI
Relationship managers (RMs) at a Malaysian bank turn long credit papers into structured fields by hand, and the first version of the extraction pipeline reached only about 30% accuracy. Ray, as POC lead engineer, fine-tuned Qwen3 to read credit papers into RM-aligned markdown and extract L1, L2 and L3 values into the right fields per the bank's standard, designed to run on limited hardware and GPU resources. Extraction accuracy rose from about 30% to 80% after he joined, and data matching exceeded 90% accuracy. He supported the sales closure with technical consultation and demos, and ran knowledge-transfer sessions and code walkthroughs for the client team.
