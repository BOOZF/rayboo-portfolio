Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026. This site is a personal portfolio. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Who runs this site

www.boozhengfeng.com is run by Ray Boo (Boo Zheng Feng), Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. For any privacy question or request, email zhengfengboo0710@gmail.com.

What is collected when you browse

Nothing is collected by the site itself. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels, and nothing is saved in your browser's storage. Fonts, images and 3D models are all served from this site, so browsing does not contact any third party.

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel automatically processes technical request data (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy.

The "Ask about Ray" assistant

If you use the chat assistant, the question you type is sent to this site's server and then to OpenAI's API, which generates the answer from facts about Ray's work. Please do not type personal or sensitive information into it.

  • Your questions and the answers are not stored by this site.
  • Your IP address is held in memory for about one minute to limit how many questions can be sent, then discarded.
  • OpenAI processes the question to produce the answer. Under its API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default and may be retained for a limited period for abuse monitoring. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

Email

If you email Ray, your message and email address are used only to reply to you and are not shared or added to any mailing list.

Links to other sites

Project pages link to live products such as Madam Kwan's mini program and RunForPeace. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

In line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you can ask what personal data is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Since the site stores no visitor data, this usually only applies to emails you have sent. Write to zhengfengboo0710@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
